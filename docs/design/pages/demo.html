Actions are judged by intentions, and every person will have only what they intended. Whoever migrated for worldly gain, or to marry a woman, their migration counts for whatever they migrated toward.

Chain of narrators

This chain is strong

Every one of the six narrators has been identified, and the classical scholar Ibn Hajar graded all of them trustworthy. No weak link was found.

Ilham reports grades that classical scholars wrote centuries ago. It does not judge whether a hadith is authentic, and a colour or a number here is never Ilham's own opinion.

Read from the top down to follow the hadith as it was passed on — the Companion who heard it first, then each person who passed it to the next, and the collector last.
